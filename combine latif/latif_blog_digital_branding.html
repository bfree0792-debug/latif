2026 Brand Audit

Does your brand pass the modern test?

  • Distinctive asset test: Cover your logo — is it still recognizable?
  • Variable type hierarchy: Custom or calibrated editorial fonts
  • Cohesive motion language: Micro-transitions with personality
  • Clear anti-persona: Knowing precisely who you are NOT for
  • Sub-second web performance: Speed as a brand statement
  • Omnichannel voice parity: Seamless sync from ad to checkout
  • Authentic founder/team presence: Human faces behind craft
  • Community gravity: Advocates defending you unprompted

05 — Verbal Differentiation

Anti-Corporate Tone:
Writing With Authority

Corporate jargon kills brands. The most valuable brands in the world speak like intelligent, confident humans:

  • ✕
    Kill the Buzzwords — Eliminate words like “synergy”, “disruptive”, “next-gen” and “end-to-end”. State plainly what you actually deliver.
  • ✓
    Take Strong Stances — If everyone in your sector agrees with your slogan, it is not a position. Pick a philosophical enemy and state why your method wins.
  • ⚡
    Editorial Microcopy — Buttons, badges, and empty states are missed branding goldmines. Make every click feel rewarding.
  • ♥
    Founder Transparency — Share behind-the-scenes decision-making, lessons from failures, and genuine trade secrets.
06 — Measurement

How to Quantify
Brand Equity

Brand is not intangible — it shows up directly on the bottom line. Track these leading indicators:

  • Branded Search Growth — Search volume for your name vs category keywords
  • Direct Traffic Share — People typing your domain straight into the browser
  • Price Inelasticity — Ability to raise rates 20–40% without losing customer volume
  • Zero-Click Attribution — “Where did you hear about us?” open-text responses
  • Talent Attraction — Inbound applications from world-class designers & engineers

10 Costly Branding
Traps in 2026

  • Copying Apple's minimalist aesthetic without Apple's product moat
  • Prioritizing virality over qualified customer resonance
  • Rebranding every 18 months instead of building equity
  • Using generic AI copywriting without a human editorial voice
  • Neglecting post-purchase packaging and onboarding UX
  • Competing on price instead of differentiated value
  • Inconsistent tone between ad creatives and website landing pages
  • Hiding founder personalities behind impersonal corporate PR
  • Choosing trendy fonts that are illegible at mobile scale
  • Treating accessibility as an afterthought rather than a core principle
