Brand Viability Checklist

Evaluate your brand concept before investing capital

  • The "Only" Test: Can you finish: "We are the ONLY company that..."?
  • Identified enemy: Clear stance against an outdated industry norm
  • Target customer avatar defined by shared values, not just age/gender
  • Domain and trademark clearance across key jurisdictions
  • Distinctive visual assets that look different from every direct competitor
  • Gross margins above 60% to support marketing and high-touch CX
  • Unboxing or digital onboarding experience that creates organic shareability
  • Clear roadmap to acquiring the first 100 passionate evangelists

04 — Implementation

The 6-Phase Zero-to-One Brand Roadmap

  • 01
    Market Whitespace Discovery
    Interview 30 prospective customers. Listen for the emotional language they use to describe their biggest frustrations.
  • 02
    Core Brand Manifesto & Naming
    Craft the brand belief system, core purpose, and a distinctive, memorable name with clean trademark clearance.
  • 03
    Design System & Visual Language
    Develop the logo mark, editorial type hierarchy, cohesive color tokens, packaging design, and brand guidelines.
  • 04
    Digital Flagship Launch (MVP)
    Engineer a fast, responsive website designed to convert early curiosity into waitlist signups and pre-orders.
  • 05
    The First 100 Customer Intensive
    Over-deliver aggressively for your earliest adopters with handwritten notes, direct founder access, and bespoke care.
  • 06
    Organic Content & Community Flywheel
    Turn customer transformations and stories into organic content engines that compound over months and years.
05 — Deep Dive

The Modern Brand Architecture

A cohesive brand requires alignment across every layer of the organization:

  • POV
    Point of View — What do you believe about the world that your competitors disagree with? Conviction attracts loyalty.
  • DSN
    Visual Distinction — Avoid generic minimalism. Use custom typography, bold color palettes, and tactile spatial design elements.
  • EXP
    Customer Experience — Fast shipping, seamless customer service, and unexpected delight moments turn buyers into brand champions.
  • STU
    Community Culture — Create rituals, terminology, and spaces where your customers connect with one another around shared identity.
06 — Metrics

Leading Indicators of Brand Health

Track these metrics from day one to validate brand-market fit:

  • Word-of-Mouth Coefficient — % of new customers originating without paid ad attribution
  • Net Promoter Score (NPS) — Willingness of buyers to enthusiastically recommend you
  • Repeat Purchase Rate — How many customers return within 90 days of first buy
  • Direct Traffic Volume — People typing your domain straight into the browser bar
  • Unboxing / UGC Mentions — Organic social shares created by proud customers

10 Fatal Startup Brand Traps

  • Focusing on logo design before validating customer problem-solution fit
  • Trying to appeal to everyone and becoming forgettable to everyone
  • Underpricing your product out of fear, destroying unit economic margins
  • Treating customer service as a cost center instead of a marketing channel
  • Copying competitor aesthetic rather than forging your own distinctive look
  • Spending money on PR before your core product experience is dialed in
  • Neglecting post-purchase email onboarding and customer education
  • Changing your core brand direction every time a new trend emerges
  • Over-promising in marketing and under-delivering in reality
  • Forgetting that a brand is built over thousands of tiny consistent decisions
